Interpretation note: Manteco garments show marginally deeper average markdowns (20.3% vs 16.6%, a 3.7pp gap). However, this finding is nuanced for three reasons: (1) Manteco's higher retail base means equivalent percentage discounts represent significantly more absolute dollar savings while still retaining greater residual value; (2) two Zara tier outliers with 25–30% discounts disproportionately inflate the Manteco average; (3) at the Contemporary Premium tier, many Manteco products remain at full price (0% markdown), compressing the average. The hypothesis is therefore mixed but not contradicted at this stage.
